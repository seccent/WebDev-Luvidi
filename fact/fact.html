Five Facts About Cats

High-Speed Runners

A house cat can run up to 30 miles per hour over short distances.

Sleepy Lifestyle

Cats spend about 70% of their lives sleeping, which usually equals 13 to 16 hours a day.

Unique Nose Prints

Every cat has a ridged nose pattern that is completely unique, much like a human fingerprint.

Tiger DNA

House cats share 95.6% of their genetic makeup with tigers and show many of the same jungle-hunting behaviors.

Meowing for Humans

Adult cats almost never meow at each other; they use meowing and chirruping primarily to communicate with people.
